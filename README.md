# Mashambani Group 

MASHAMBANI GROUP is a diversified Kenyan enterprise operating across agriculture, agro-inputs, chemicals, manufacturing, logistics, storage solutions, and food processing.

We provide quality products and innovative solutions designed to improve productivity, efficiency, and value across the agricultural and industrial value chains.

Our businesses include agrochemicals and agricultural inputs, animal health and veterinary products,industrial and specialty chemicals, logistics and transportation, hermetic bag manufacturing, and maize milling and value addition.

By connecting farm inputs, production, storage, logistics, processing, and distribution, 

Mashambani Group is building an integrated business ecosystem that creates value for farmers, businesses, consumers, and communities.

Driven by quality, innovation, reliability, and sustainable growth, We are committed to becoming a trusted partner across the markets we serve.
